How it works
01
Join with your code
Enter the 6-digit code from your host and pick a display name. You start with ₹1,50,000 cash — borrow more from a bank if you want extra firepower.
02
Pick your bank
3 banks to borrow from — Bharat Bank, VentureCapX, ShadowCredit. Interest of 1% to 12% is charged on your loans at the end of every round.
03
Read the market
20 companies across 14 sectors, plus 2 safe-route funds. Two more companies list later in the game — keep an eye out.
04
Trade in 6 rounds
6 rounds of 20 minutes each, with a 10-minute break between them. Buy and sell before the timer runs out — and watch the news.
05
Win by net worth
Cash + portfolio value − loan balance at the end of the final round. Highest net worth wins.
