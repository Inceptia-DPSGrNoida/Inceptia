How it works
01
Join with your code
Enter the 6-digit code from your host and pick a display name. You start with ₹1,50,000 cash — borrow more from a bank if you want extra firepower.
02
Pick your bank
3 banks to borrow from — Bharat Bank, VentureCapX, ShadowCredit. Each has different rates and limits.

Read the market
8 companies across 8 sectors, plus 2 safe-route funds. Two companies list mid-game as fresh New Listings — keep an eye out.
04
Trade in 4 rounds
4 rounds of 20 minutes each, with a 10-minute break between them. Buy and sell before the timer runs out.
05
Win by net worth
Cash + portfolio value − loan balance at end of the final round, after tax. Highest net worth wins.
